Catalog save recovery

Remote cards and their automatic tag metadata are committed together in chunks of at most 64 rows. Accepted non-destructive deltas have a versioned pending record; applying data and removing that record share one IndexedDB transaction with the commit marker. Remote catalog metadata advances only after its accepted row chunks finish. Follow observations retain their existing independent ledger. Recovery scans pending operations, not the full library. Unknown recovery versions are preserved and block conflicting catalog writes.

Manual tags, provenance, locks and category share one small recovery record. A synchronous combined mirror covers a just-edited tab close; IndexedDB is the primary durable path. A lost acknowledgement can replay without duplicating titles or personal counters. If both storage mechanisms reject a new edit, it remains unsaved in the visit and requires an explicit save retry. No system can persist new data when all available storage refuses writes.

Cleanup retains its existing atomic transaction. An interrupted cleanup leaves the prior catalog intact and does not schedule a surprise deletion for later. Existing saved metadata, history, feedback, follows, sources, drafts and media are protected.

Storage recovery offers a read-only preview of at most 256 browser artwork entries. Confirmation deletes only those disposable entries whose timestamp and payload size still match. Changed or missing entries are skipped. Cancellation removes nothing. Byte totals describe stored image payload estimates, not measured filesystem space. Browser origin estimates include other data and may be unavailable. Normal browsing can refill artwork; offline artwork can disappear until its source is available. Original media and native caches are never cleared here.

Retry is explicit. It waits for initial recovery, then recovers accepted operations and saves current library preferences and currently visible remote cards in batches without advancing source metadata or deleting legacy snapshots without opening a provider or starting downloads. An import stopped before durable acceptance may need to be repeated after storage is available. It does not claim that every independent feature draft has been externally published or every device has verified its media. Journal transactions request strict IndexedDB durability where supported; physical power-loss and browser eviction guarantees are outside the injected interruption checks. Cross-tab writer leasing, atomic whole-library restore and profile isolation remain separate milestones.
